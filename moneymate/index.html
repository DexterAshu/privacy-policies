Privacy Policy

MoneyMate — Your Everyday Financial Companion

Effective Date: September 30, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to MoneyMate ("MoneyMate", "we", "our", or "the app"). MoneyMate is a financial calculator and planning application designed to help users perform everyday financial calculations and planning.

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information MoneyMate handles, how it is used, and how your information is protected.

2. Information We Collect

MoneyMate is designed to operate primarily on your device and does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use its core calculation features.

Financial Calculation Inputs

You may enter information such as loan amounts, investment amounts, salary figures, tax inputs, financial goals, and other values required to perform calculations. These values are used to generate results within the app.

Saved Calculations and Preferences

If you choose to save calculations, favorites, recent calculations, theme preferences, or other app settings, this information may be stored locally on your device.

MoneyMate does not require you to submit your financial calculation inputs to a MoneyMate server for its core calculator functionality.

3. How We Use Information

Information entered into MoneyMate is used to:

  • Perform financial calculations requested by you.
  • Display calculation results and summaries.
  • Save calculations when you choose to use the save feature.
  • Remember preferences such as your selected theme.
  • Provide backup and restore functionality when initiated by you.
  • Provide and improve your experience within the application.

4. Data Storage

MoneyMate is designed with an offline-first approach. Saved calculations, favorites, recent items, preferences, and related app data may be stored locally on your device using the app's local storage mechanisms.

MoneyMate does not operate a user account system for its core features and does not require cloud storage of your saved calculations.

5. Backup and Restore

MoneyMate may provide a backup and restore feature that allows you to export or import your locally stored app data.

Any backup file created by you is under your control. Please store backup files securely and do not share them with people you do not trust, as they may contain information you entered into the application.

6. Data Sharing and Sale

MoneyMate does not sell your personal information.

The core application is designed not to transmit your financial calculation inputs to MoneyMate servers for processing.

We do not use your financial calculation inputs for advertising profiling or targeted advertising.

7. Third-Party Services

The core MoneyMate calculator functionality is designed to work without requiring third-party financial processing services.

If future versions introduce third-party services, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, cloud synchronization, or other external processing, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate before those features are introduced.

8. Permissions

MoneyMate only requests device permissions when they are required for a specific feature. Permission availability and behavior may vary by Android version and device.

You can review or change application permissions through your device's Android settings.

9. Children's Privacy

MoneyMate is a general-purpose financial calculator application and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the core functionality of the application.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information handled by the application. Because locally stored data is maintained on your device, you should also use appropriate device security, such as a screen lock, device encryption, and secure handling of exported backup files.

11. Your Control Over Your Data

You can manage or remove locally stored MoneyMate data using the application's available data-management features or by clearing the application's storage through your device settings.

Clearing application data may permanently remove locally stored calculations, favorites, preferences, and other app data that has not been backed up.

12. Financial Information Disclaimer

MoneyMate provides calculation and planning tools for informational and educational purposes. Results are estimates based on the information and assumptions entered by the user.

Actual loan, investment, tax, salary, interest, and other financial outcomes may vary based on applicable laws, regulations, financial institutions, products, fees, rates, and individual circumstances.

MoneyMate does not provide personalized financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. Please consult an appropriately qualified professional where necessary.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, its features, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published at this location with a revised effective date.
